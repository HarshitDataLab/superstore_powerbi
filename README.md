# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes sales, profit, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and shipping operations using the Superstore dataset. The dashboard provides interactive visualizations and business insights to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions and improve overall business performance.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on profit.
* Understand customer and regional performance.
* Analyze shipping modes and order fulfillment trends.
* Generate actionable business insights.

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales & Profit Dashboard

Key KPIs:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Profit Margin

Insights:

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Monthly Profit Trend
* Sales by Category
* Profit by Category
* Overall Business Performance

---

### 2. Product & Discount Analysis

Insights:

* Sales by Product Category
* Sales by Sub-Category
* Top Products by Sales
* Discount Impact on Profit
* Product Performance Analysis

Key Questions:

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* How do discounts affect profitability?

---

### 3. Regional & Customer Insights

Insights:

* Sales by Region
* Profit by Region
* Customer Segment Analysis
* Top Customers by Sales
* Regional Performance Comparison

Purpose:

* Identify high-performing regions and valuable customer segments.

---

### 4. Shipping Mode Analysis

Insights:

* Orders by Ship Mode
* Sales by Ship Mode
* Profit by Ship Mode
* Shipping Performance Evaluation

Key Questions:

* Which shipping mode is most frequently used?
* Which shipping mode generates the highest profit?

---

### 5. Executive Summary

Business Highlights:

## Key Insights

* Technology category generated the highest sales revenue.
* Certain high-discount products negatively impacted profitability.
* Regional sales performance varied significantly across markets.
* A small percentage of customers contributed a large share of total revenue.
* Standard Class was the most commonly used shipping mode.
