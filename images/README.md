# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built using the Superstore dataset. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and shipping efficiency.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Understand regional and customer trends
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability
- Analyze shipping modes and delivery performance
- Provide actionable business insights through visual analytics

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales & Profit Dashboard
Key KPIs:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin

Insights:
- Sales and profit trends over time
- Category-wise performance
- Top-performing segments

### 2. Product & Discount Analysis
Insights:
- Sales by Product Category

- Top Products by Revenue
- Impact of Discounts on Profitability
- Product Performance Comparison

### 3. Regional & Customer Insights
Insights:
- Sales by Region

- Customer Segment Analysis
- Top Customers by Sales
- Regional Performance Comparison

---

### 4. Shipping Mode Analysis
Insights:
- Orders by Ship Mode
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Profit by Ship Mode
- Shipping Performance Evaluation

---

### 5. Executive Summary
Business Highlights:
- Overall business performance overview

- High-performing regions and products

## Key Business Insights
- Technology category generated the highest sales.
- Certain high-discount products reduced profitability.
- Regional sales performance varied significantly.
- Standard Class accounted for the majority of shipments.
- A small group of customers contributed a large share of revenue.
